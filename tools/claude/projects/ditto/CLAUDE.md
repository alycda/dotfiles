## Company Values

First principles
We reason from reality and constraints, not precedent, politics, or comfort.

Deliver trust
Trust is earned by delivery: ship what you commit to, fix what you break.

Truth early
We surface issues fast and make the next step explicit. We’re direct, with positive intent.

Own it
One owner. Clear DRIs. Fast escalation. Finish the work.

Heroics, then harden
Step up when it matters, then harden the system so it doesn’t need heroics again.

High standards, high care
We push hard and support each other, but we don’t tolerate sustained mediocrity.

Mission first
We choose the hardest, highest-stakes problems we can win and say no to distractions. We build for unstoppable operations.
